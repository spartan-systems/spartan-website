Questions

Trial, plans and data

Other questions: sales@spartansystems.us

Does the trial need a card?

No. New accounts get 7 days of any plan with no card.

What happens when the trial ends?

Add a card to keep the plan. Without a card, the account moves to the free tier and nothing is charged.

What does the free tier include?

NSN and solicitation lookups, alerts by email, text and in the app, SAM.gov notices, DLA forecasts and lead times, your dashboard and 3 plain-English searches a day.

Where does the data come from?

DIBBS, DLA's FOIA files, FLIS, DLA's CAGE file and SAM.gov.

How current are solicitations?

The DIBBS feed is live. About 1,900 new solicitations post each business day.

Does Spartan submit quotes on DIBBS?

No. The bid board builds one DIBBS batch quote file. You upload it on DIBBS.

How many users does a plan cover?

1, 3 or 5. Every plan includes every feature.

How do I cancel?

Settings, Subscription, Manage Billing. The plan stays active to the end of the paid month.

Is there an API?

Yes, on every plan. Documentation is at docs.spartansystems.us.
