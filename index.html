Leo

Query the data in plain English.

Leo is Spartan's AI assistant. It answers from the same award, solicitation and stock data as the rest of the platform, including your inventory, alerts and bid board, and shows how it reached each answer.

Asked in Spartan on November 5, 2026
Who are the top vendors for titanium bolts and screws over the last 2 years, and what do they charge?
CAGE 1HEN9 leads by a wide margin: 67 awards across 9 items, averaging $10.98 each, with prices from $3.35 to $29.29. Next are CAGE 2V484 with 9 awards at $6.60 on average and CAGE 01UA8 with 7 at $43.59.
Which open solicitations for socket head cap screws close in the next 7 days?
46 open lines across 45 solicitations. 43 are unrestricted, two are Small Business set-asides and one is SDVOSB. The largest is SPE4A627T7844, at 3,355 and 10,243 each on two lines.
What is NSN 5306-01-045-6594 made of, and what has DLA paid for it?
Steel. The latest award paid $8.50 each in October 2025, and prices have ranged from $3.19 to $10.66 across 12 awards. Small lots seem to carry a premium: 47 units at $8.50 against 303 units at $5.86 a month earlier.
