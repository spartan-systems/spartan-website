AI Assistant

Ask any question and get an expert answer in plain English — instantly.

Spartan includes a built-in AI assistant that knows the entire DoD procurement database inside and out. Ask it anything — from basic questions about a part to detailed material specifications — and get a clear, sourced answer in seconds.

No more digging through government spec sheets. No more calling your procurement consultant for every small question. Just ask.

Real examples of what you can ask:
"What is NSN 5330-00-485-0788 made out of?"
This is a plain seal under FSC 5330 (packing and gasket materials). Based on its specification data, it is made from nitrile rubber — a synthetic material used in military hydraulic and fuel systems because of its resistance to petroleum-based fluids and extreme temperatures.
"Are there any open solicitations right now for stainless steel parts?"
Yes — I found 14 open solicitations specifying stainless steel construction, including 3 sole-source opportunities in FSC 4730 with bids closing within the next 30 days. Want me to sort them by deadline or dollar value?
"Who are the biggest vendors for titanium fasteners?"
Over the last 24 months, the top vendors for titanium fasteners in FSC 5306 are CAGE 66200 ($1.28M in awards), CAGE 3B558 ($659K), and CAGE 48T42 ($438K). The most recent award was in December 2024 at $12.40 per unit.

Things your team can ask the AI:

  • What material or specification a specific NSN requires
  • Open solicitations filtered by material type or military spec
  • Which companies are the top vendors in any product category
  • What a fair price is for any part number before you bid
  • Which set-aside categories you qualify for on any contract
  • Historical delivery times and lead time data
  • How to cross-reference a part number to find the right NSN
  • Anything a 30-year procurement expert would know
