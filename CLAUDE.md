# Spartan Systems Marketing Site

Static marketing site (home page, privacy policy, terms of service and SMS terms) served by GitHub Pages at **spartansystems.us** (apex domain via `CNAME` file). No build step, no framework, no dependencies.

- `privacy/index.html` is the privacy policy (served at `/privacy/`), linked from the footer here and from the app. Keep it in step with what the product actually collects and which providers receive data.
- `terms/index.html` is the Terms of Service (`/terms/`), linked from every footer and given to Twilio as the toll-free verification's terms URL. Keep its plans, trial and cancelling text in step with the pricing section and the app's billing, and its SMS section in step with `/sms/`.
- `sms/index.html` is the SMS Alerts Terms (`/sms/`), the public opt-in proof for Twilio toll-free verification, linked from the app's consent box. Its consent text, confirmation and HELP replies must match the app's Notifications section and spartan-api's `SmsProgram`; the `sms/opt-in-*.png` screenshots show the app's opt-in steps.
- Everything else lives in `index.html`: inline CSS (custom properties like `--navy`, `--blue2`), one inline script (IntersectionObserver reveal animations + screenshot tab switcher). External resources are Google Fonts (Inter), the Google Ads tag (`AW-17734229904`, in the `<head>` of every page, ad personalization off; the policy's cookies section depends on that), and PostHog (same project and token as the app, `cross_subdomain_cookie` so data.spartansystems.us continues the visitor's ID; covered by the policy's PostHog lines).
- All CTAs point to the product app: Log In to `https://data.spartansystems.us/login`; the trial buttons to `https://data.spartansystems.us/start-trial` (government-data's route: Create Account when signed out, then the plan picker after onboarding); the hero's NSN box to `/search?nsn=`; contact is `mailto:sales@spartansystems.us`. Each trial button carries `data-ph-capture-attribute-placement` for PostHog.
- The hero tile and the two DIBBS coverage cards fill in dated counts from spartan-api's anonymous `GET /api/Site/stats` (CORS allows this origin for that route only). If the call fails the static text stays.
- The `screen_*.webp` screenshots are of the `government-data` app and go stale when its UI changes. The 2026-11-05 set was taken as the QA account through a read-only proxy to the prod API, personalized to CAGE 6JSD9, a small fastener supplier. Its name is kept out, but the CAGE shows on the dashboard, the pricing vendor table and Leo's answer, since all three are public award data. The bid board is made up from that CAGE's real open solicitations and DIBBS awards, with closed lines in the automatic "Awarded on DIBBS" state. Leo's answers on the page are real, asked that day and shortened. `screen_pricing.webp` was retaken on 2026-11-06 (SPAR-218, the confidence chip removed) on the live app as the QA account, same NSN 5306-01-045-6594 and framing. The NSN page doesn't read the user's CAGE, so that shot needs no proxy.
- Deploy = push to the default branch (Pages serves the branch directly; there is no Actions workflow).
- `clip_*.mp4` are muted loops (Leo, bid board quote file, search to NSN pricing) recorded 2026-11-13 with the same QA proxy and CAGE 6JSD9: headless Chrome screencast at 2x, cropped to one panel, encoded to H.264 by Chrome's MediaRecorder and remuxed with macOS `avconvert -p PresetPassthrough` so the file has a duration. Each is under 1 MB; `clip_*.webp` is its poster. The page's script plays a clip only while it is on screen and leaves it paused with controls under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
